## Locally at JMU{{attrs[#blk-cny0lc5eoiax]}}

### https://www.lib.jmu.edu/open-ed/

Using these webpages as a guide, you can search for OER, learn about Creative Commons licenses, and find out about adapting and creating your own open educational resources.

- In a https://media.lib.jmu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-JMU-Course-Material-Affordability-2.pdf, JMU students reported that the cost of course materials caused them to:

- **Not register for a course:** 37%

- **Take fewer courses:** 29%

- **Earn a poor grade:** 32%

- CFI Teaching Toolbox (2020): https://www.jmu.edu/news/cfi/2020/07-13-open-educational-resources.shtml

JMU complies with federal and state laws that require faculty to identify required course materials in advance through the https://www.bkstr.com/jamesmadisonstore/shop/textbooks-and-course-materials and https://mymadison.jmu.edu/.

While students may purchase materials anywhere, JMU is https://www.congress.gov/bill/110th-congress/house-bill/4137 by the https://www.congress.gov/bill/110th-congress/house-bill/4137 to provide price information so students can anticipate required course costs in advance.

https://www.jmu.edu/jmu-policy/policies/pdfs/2110.pdf also details JMU’s commitment to using https://www.lib.jmu.edu/open-ed/ where possible.

### Virginia Legislature

#### https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/23.1-1308/

- **https://lis.virginia.gov/cgi-bin/legp604.exe?181+sum+HB454S**

Requires the governing board of each public institution of higher education to implement guidelines for the adoption and use of low-cost and no-cost open educational resources in courses offered at such institutions. The bill provides that such guidelines may include provisions for low-cost commercially published materials.

- **https://lis.virginia.gov/cgi-bin/legp604.exe?191+ful+HB2380**

Requires the registrar or another appropriate employee of each public institution of higher education to identify conspicuously in the online course catalog or registration system, as soon as practicable after the necessary information becomes available, each course for which the instructor exclusively uses no-cost course materials or low-cost course materials.

### https://sparcopen.org/open-education/

- **https://sparcopen.org/our-work/sparc-library-oer-forum/:** Vibrant community of practice for library professionals working in open education, including a discussion list and monthly calls.

- **https://sparcopen.org/our-work/state-policy-tracking/:** Check out the OER State Policy Tracker or download the https://sparcopen.org/our-work/oer-state-policy-playbook/.

- **https://sparcopen.org/our-work/oer-mythbusting/:** Resource developed to dispel the most common OER myths.
