# Locally at JMU

## [Open Education at JMU Guide](https://www.lib.jmu.edu/open-ed/){{attrs[#blk-3gqnefoechw5]}}

Using the webpages as a guide, you can search for OER, learn about Creative Commons licenses, and find out about adapting and creating your own open educational resources.

- In a [2025 student survey](https://media.lib.jmu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-JMU-Course-Material-Affordability-2.pdf), JMU students reported that the cost of course materials caused them to:
  - **Not register for a course** (37%)
  - **Take fewer courses** (29%)
  - **Earn a poor grade** (32%)

- CFI Teaching Toolbox (2020): **[Open Educational Resources: Getting the Most from Your Course Materials](https://www.jmu.edu/news/cfi/2020/07-13-open-educational-resources.shtml)**

JMU complies with federal and state laws that require faculty to identify required course materials in advance through the [bookstore website](https://www.bkstr.com/jamesmadisonstore/shop/textbooks-and-course-materials) and [MyMadison](https://mymadison.jmu.edu/). While students may purchase materials anywhere, JMU is [legally required](https://www.congress.gov/bill/110th-congress/house-bill/4137) by the Higher Education Opportunity Act to provide price information so that students can anticipate the required costs of a class in advance.

[JMU Policy 2110: Ordering Textbooks](https://www.jmu.edu/jmu-policy/policies/pdfs/2110.pdf) also details JMU's commitment to using [Open Educational Resources](https://www.lib.jmu.edu/open-ed/) (OER) where possible.

## [VIVA Open Grants](https://vivalib.org/va/open/grants){{attrs[#blk-d4e6bedk3577]}}

VIVA (Virginia's Academic Library Consortia) provides grant funding for adopting, adapting, and creating open materials for courses at Virginia institutions of higher education.

## Virginia Legislature{{attrs[#blk-643377deqev1]}}

### [§ 23.1-1308. Governing board procedures; textbook sales and bookstores; open educational resources](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/23.1-1308/)

- **[HB454 (2018)](https://lis.virginia.gov/cgi-bin/legp604.exe?181+sum+HB454S)**

  "Requires the governing board of each public institution of higher education to implement guidelines for the adoption and use of low-cost and no-cost open educational resources in courses offered at such institution. The bill provides that such guidelines may include provisions for low-cost commercially published materials."

- **[HB2380 (2019)](https://lis.virginia.gov/cgi-bin/legp604.exe?191+ful+HB2380)**

  "Requires the registrar or another appropriate employee of each public institution of higher education to identify conspicuously in the online course catalog or registration system, as soon as practicable after the necessary information becomes available, each course for which the instructor exclusively uses no-cost course materials or low-cost course materials."

# The OER Community

## [Open Education Network](https://open.umn.edu/otn/){{attrs[#blk-dqmkzswttkdf]}}

As a member of the Open Education Network, JMU contributes textbooks and textbook reviews to the **[Open Textbook Library](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks)**.

## [SPARC's Open Education Work](https://sparcopen.org/open-education/){{attrs[#blk-t1yjxhn67wgb]}}

- **[LibOER Discussion Group](https://sparcopen.org/our-work/sparc-library-oer-forum/):** A vibrant community of practice of library professionals working in open education, including a discussion list and monthly calls.

- **[Connect OER](https://sparcopen.org/our-work/connect-oer/):** Join the platform to share and discover information about OER activities across North America and read the annual report.

- **[OER State Policy Resources](https://sparcopen.org/our-work/state-policy-tracking/):** Check out the OER State Policy Tracker for the latest developments, or download the [OER State Policy Playbook](https://sparcopen.org/our-work/oer-state-policy-playbook/).

- **[OER Digest](https://sparcopen.org/our-work/oer-digest/):** SPARC co-authors this bi-weekly newsletter for open education updates, opportunities, and reminders.

- **[OER Mythbusting](https://sparcopen.org/our-work/oer-mythbusting/):** A collaboratively developed resource dispelling the most common OER myths.
