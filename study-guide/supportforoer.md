# Support for OER

## Locally at JMU{{attrs[#blk-tb9piwp54esv]}}

### [Open Education at JMU](https://www.lib.jmu.edu/open-ed/)

Using these webpages as a guide, you can search for OER, learn about the Creative Commons licenses, and find out about adapting and creating your own open educational resources.

- In a [2025 student survey](https://media.lib.jmu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-JMU-Course-Material-Affordability-2.pdf), JMU students reported that the cost of course materials caused them to:
  - **Not register for a course** (37%)
  - **Take fewer courses** (29%)
  - **Earn a poor grade** (32%)

- CFI Teaching Toolbox (2020): [Open Educational Resources: Getting the Most from your Course materials](https://www.jmu.edu/news/cfi/2020/07-13-open-educational-resources.shtml)

JMU complies with federal and state laws which require faculty to identify required course materials in advance through the [bookstore website](https://www.bkstr.com/jamesmadisonstore/shop/textbooks-and-course-materials) and [MyMadison](https://mymadison.jmu.edu/). While students may purchase materials anywhere, JMU is legally required by the [Higher Education Opportunity Act](https://www.congress.gov/bill/110) to provide price information so that students can anticipate the required costs of a class in advance. [JMU Policy 2110: Ordering Textbooks](https://www.jmu.edu/jmu-policy/policies/pdfs/2110.pdf) also details JMU's committment to using [Open Educational Resources (OER)](https://www.lib.jmu.edu/open-ed/) where possible.

## Locally in Virginia{{attrs[#blk-o3zn92snr173]}}

### [VIVA Open Grants](https://vivalib.org/va/open/grants)

VIVA (Virginia's Academic Library Consortium) provides grant funding for adopting, adapting, and creating open materials for courses at Virginia institutions of higher education.

- [VIVA Open Adopt Grants](https://vivalib.org/va/open/grants/adopt)
- [VIVA Open Course Grants](https://vivalib.org/va/open/grants/course)
- [VIVA Grants for Libraries](https://vivalib.org/va/open/grants/libraries)

### Virginia Legislature

#### [Virginia Code § 23.1-1308: Governing Board Procedures; Textbook Sales and Bookstores; Open Educational Resources](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/23.1-1308/)
- **[HB454 (2018)](https://lis.virginia.gov/cgi-bin/legp604.exe?181+sum+HB454S)** Requires the board of each public institution of higher education to implement guides for the adoption and use of low-cost and no-cost open educational resources in courses offered at such institutions. The bill provides that such guidelines may include provisions for low-cost commercially published materials.

- **[HB2380 (2019)](https://lis.virginia.gov/cgi-bin/legp604.exe?191+ful+HB2380)** 
  Requires the registrar or another appropriate employee of each public institution of higher education to identify conspicuously in the online course catalog or registration system, as soon as practicable after the necessary information becomes available, each course for which the instructor exclusively uses no-cost course materials or low-cost course materials.
